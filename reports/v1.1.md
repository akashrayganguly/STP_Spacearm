## v1.1 — Writeup, interactive explorer, stage and variant studies: DONE

Built:
* `docs/WRITEUP.md` — illustrated, story-style tour of every model (about 3,100 words of prose, 20 figures, tables for every variant). It folds in the questions from the v1.0 walkthrough (FK is exact geometry, not learned; control points are not path steps; refine is test-time optimisation through a frozen DistanceNet; and so on).
* `src/spacearm/gui/` + `scripts/gui.py` — Gradio web app:
  * **① pose and clearance:** true `d_body`/`d_self` vs DistanceNet, with the closest link pairs drawn;
  * **② path planner:** every stage checked in PyBullet, with clearance and control-point charts;
  * **③ mission simulator:** choice of controller (or all four), start, target, ball (none/static/drifting, size, timing, speed), encoder bias, motor weakness, slip and noise; presets S1–S4 plus a stress test; replay of any report episode;
  * a WebM video, a time-series chart and a 3D replay.
* `src/spacearm/envs/space_reach_env.py` — `reset(options=...)` pins start, target, obstacle, faults and noise (additive; default episodes unchanged).
* `scripts/eval_level1_stages.py` → `reports/level1/stages.{json,md}` — the planner stage by stage on the v1.0 query sets.
* `scripts/eval_level2_variants.py` → `reports/level2/variants/*.json`, `reports/level2/variants.md` — 17 variants × 4 methods × 100 episodes.
* `scripts/make_writeup_figures.py` → `reports/figures/writeup/*.png`.

Decisions / deviations and why:
* **No DESIGN or config numbers changed.** The environment and planner changes are additive and covered by tests.
* **New dependencies, for the GUI and figures only:** gradio, plotly and kaleido. They are added to `environment.yml`, `cloud/setup.sh` and `pyproject.toml` (extra `[gui]`); the core package does not import them.
* **Timings:** this VM ran about 1.7× slower than the v1.0 machine, so the stage-study times are relative. The writeup quotes v1.0's absolute planner time (99 ms).
* **Fig. 10 honesty fix:** a 2D side view made the reflex's tip look as if it went through its ball, while the true 3D clearance stayed at 6 cm or more. The panel was removed; the episode figure shows only true distances.
